Kigencho

Expiry Date Tracker

日本語

Kigencho is an iPhone app that saves the best-before or use-by date of food when you point the camera at the package, and reminds you before it expires.

Features

  • Point the camera at the printed date and AI reads the product name and the date
  • One notification each morning lists the food that expires soon
  • The list is grouped by how soon items expire, with filters for fridge, freezer and pantry
  • Mark items as eaten or thrown away and review each month
  • Records and photos are stored only on your iPhone
